About Dr. Lama Ghassan

Who is Dr. Lama Ghassan?

Dr. Lama Ghassan Shreba is a dentist (BDS) specialising in aesthetic and restorative dentistry, with more than 10 years of clinical experience. She practised in Lattakia, Syria, before bringing her work to Oman, where she now treats patients at NHC — Dr. Nael Al Hazeem Medical Center in Muscat.

Her clinical work has been presented at the 23rd Annual Meeting of the European Society of Cosmetic Dentistry (ESCD) in Bucharest, and she has spoken about smile design on Oman TV. “The best smile is the one that looks naturally yours — quality is in the details.”
