Aesthetic & Restorative Dentistry

Explore Our Treatments

Every smile begins with listening. A careful examination, a plan designed around your face and goals, and minimally invasive techniques that protect your natural teeth.
Porcelain Veneers
Ultra-thin feldspathic ceramic veneers that reshape and brighten teeth while keeping them natural.
Porcelain veneers are thin ceramic shells bonded to the front of the teeth to change their colour, shape and length. Dr. Lama favours ultra-thin feldspathic ceramic, hand-layered for natural translucency — so your smile looks bright and beautiful without looking artificial.
Read More
Hollywood Smile
A complete, balanced smile makeover — bright, symmetrical and designed to suit your face.
A Hollywood smile combines veneers, crowns, whitening and gum shaping into one carefully planned makeover. Instead of a one-size-fits-all white, each tooth is designed to suit your face, lips and skin tone — sometimes only minor adjustments are needed for a completely different smile.
Read More
Digital Smile Design
Plan and preview your new smile digitally before any treatment begins.
Using photos, videos and digital planning, Dr. Lama maps out your ideal tooth proportions before any treatment. You can see and discuss the proposed result in advance, so the final smile matches what you imagined.
Read More
No-Prep Veneers (Lumineers)
Wafer-thin veneers bonded with little or no drilling — gentle on your natural teeth.
Lumineers and other no-prep veneers are wafer-thin restorations bonded directly onto the tooth surface with little or no drilling. They are ideal for front teeth that need a better shape or colour while keeping your natural enamel protected.
Read More
Composite Bonding
Tooth-coloured resin sculpted by hand to repair chips, edges and small gaps in one visit.
Composite bonding uses tooth-coloured resin, sculpted and polished by hand, to repair chips, lengthen short teeth or soften uneven edges. It is quick, conservative and usually completed in a single visit.
Read More
Gap Closure (Diastema)
Close gaps between front teeth with veneers or bonding for a seamless smile.
Gaps between the front teeth (diastema) can be closed with veneers or bonding, often without braces. Each tooth is reshaped in proportion so the result looks seamless and natural, never crowded.
Read More
Teeth Whitening
Professional whitening that lifts stains safely for a brighter, natural shade.
Professional whitening safely lifts stains from coffee, tea and time. Dr. Lama chooses a shade that brightens your smile while still looking natural, and advises you on keeping it white for longer.
Read More
Zirconia & E.max Crowns
Strong, life-like all-ceramic crowns that restore damaged or heavily filled teeth.
All-ceramic crowns made from zirconia or E.max restore teeth that are broken, heavily filled or root-treated. They are strong enough for everyday chewing and crafted to match the colour and translucency of your other teeth.
Read More
Tooth-Coloured Fillings
Invisible composite fillings that restore decayed teeth and blend with your enamel.
Tooth-coloured composite fillings replace decay or old dark fillings with a restoration that blends invisibly with your enamel, preserving as much healthy tooth structure as possible.
Read More
Gum Contouring
Reshapes an uneven or gummy gum line to frame your teeth in perfect proportion.
Gum contouring gently reshapes an uneven or high gum line so the teeth appear in better proportion. It is often combined with veneers to frame a new smile perfectly.
Read More
Inlays & Onlays
Precise ceramic restorations that rebuild teeth conservatively and last for years.
Inlays and onlays are custom ceramic pieces that rebuild larger cavities or damaged cusps. They are more conservative than crowns and give a strong, long-lasting, natural-looking repair.
Read More
Full-Mouth Rehabilitation
A staged plan that restores function, bite and beauty across the whole smile.
When several teeth are worn, broken or missing, full-mouth rehabilitation restores comfortable chewing and a healthy bite together with beautiful aesthetics — planned in clear, manageable stages.
Read More
Dental Check-up & Cleaning
Regular exams and professional cleaning to keep your smile healthy.
Regular examinations and professional cleaning keep teeth and gums healthy and protect every cosmetic restoration you invest in. Problems are found early, when treatment is simplest.
Read More
Consultation & Smile Plan
An honest assessment and a clear, personalised treatment plan.
Your journey begins with a consultation. Dr. Lama listens to what you would like to change, examines your teeth and explains your options clearly — with a personalised plan and no pressure.
