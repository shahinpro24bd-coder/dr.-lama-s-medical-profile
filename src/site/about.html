About Dr. Lama Ghassan

Who is Dr. Lama Ghassan?

Cosmetic Dentist | Dr. Lama Ghassan — Aesthetic & Restorative Dentistry

BDS · 10+ Years of Experience

Dr. Lama Ghassan Shreba is a dentist (BDS) who has dedicated more than ten years to aesthetic and restorative dentistry. She began her career in Lattakia, Syria, and today treats patients from across Oman at NHC — Dr. Nael Al Hazeem Medical Center in Muscat.

Her work centres on veneers and smile design: ultra-thin feldspathic ceramic veneers, no-prep Lumineers, composite bonding and complete Hollywood smile makeovers — always planned around the patient's face, lips and bite.

She presented her clinical work “Form, Space & Harmony” at the 23rd Annual Meeting of the European Society of Cosmetic Dentistry (ESCD) in Bucharest, Romania, and has appeared on Oman TV and radio to talk about natural, confident smiles.

Patients often describe her as calm, attentive and gentle — many who were once afraid of the dentist say a single visit changed their perspective.

Her philosophy is simple: quality lives in the details, and the best smile is the one that looks naturally yours.
